Through your member account, you may book any classes available in our fitness studios during the scheduled class times. Attendance at a class is only possible with prior registration. Training sessions and classes must be canceled at least 12 hours in advance. Cancellations made later cannot be accepted. Refunds are no longer possible in such cases. After this deadline, or in the event of a no-show, the booked class will still be deducted from your class balance unless a third party agrees to participate at short notice.

You should arrive in your class room 10 minutes before the start of training. If you arrive later, your spot may already have been given to a member on the waiting list. In such cases, the class will not be charged.

Participants attending a training session/class for the first time should arrive in the class room 15 minutes before the start of training. Trainers will provide an introduction to the training and class process before the class begins.

We reserve the right to cancel classes at short notice and to change schedules and/or trainers. If there are fewer than five participants, we reserve the right not to hold the class. Registered participants will be informed of cancellations by email at least 10 hours in advance. The canceled session will be credited back to the participants.

No classes take place between December 24 and January 2. Furthermore, we reserve the right to close on all public holidays and for up to 9 days per year due to renovation work or other urgent operational requirements without entitling members to claim refunds. Dates will be announced in advance via notice and/or email.

House Rules

  • Please inform your trainer before each training session of any injuries, health issues, or physical limitations. The trainer will inform you whether participation in the class is safely possible and under what conditions.
  • Towels are required during training. We provide one towel per training session. After training, towels must be placed in the designated containers. Towels remain the property of Classy Pilates.
  • Do not deviate from your trainer’s instructions. Incorrect or deviating execution of exercises poses a risk of injury.
  • Always bring your treadmill to a complete stop before stepping off the machine.
  • Do not place objects such as weights or similar items on the treadmill.
  • All equipment used during training must be returned to its designated place after the training session.
