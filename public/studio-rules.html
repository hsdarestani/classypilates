Studio Rules

Official information and policies

Before class

Arrive early

Please arrive 10–15 minutes before class to settle in, center yourself, and prepare for your practice. Late arrivals may not be admitted to avoid disrupting others.

Check in at reception

Sign in at the front desk.

New to the studio?

If it’s your first visit, let the instructors know.

A quiet sanctuary

Help us maintain a calm, meditative environment by keeping voices low and silencing your phone. If you wear a smartwatch, kindly turn off audio notifications.

Shoes & belongings

No shoes, bags, or personal belongings are allowed inside the practice room—only water and a towel. Please store everything else on the reception shelves.

Health & safety

If you have any injuries, medical conditions, or physical limitations, please inform your instructor before class so we can support you safely.

During & after сlass

Commit to the full experience

For your benefit and the cohesion of the class, please stay for the full duration.
If you absolutely must leave early, inform your instructor before class so your exit can be coordinated discreetly.

Towels required

A towel is essential for comfort and hygiene. Please bring your own, or you may purchase one at the studio.

Respect the space

Keep noise to a minimum and avoid disruptive behavior. Allow yourself and others to be fully present.

Mindful movement

Be aware of your surroundings and respect the personal space and energy of others practicing nearby.

Clean your space

Please wipe down any equipment or mats you used after the class. Cleaning materials are provided for your convenience.

Take a moment

Feel free to sit, breathe, and integrate your practice before moving on with your day.

Additional notes

If you are running late, we may not be able to open the studio door or permit entry to ensure a peaceful class experience.

Photos may be taken before or after class only, never during practice.

By honouring these guidelines, you help create a supportive, respectful, and uplifting community for everyone who walks through our doors. Thank you for being part of our studio.
